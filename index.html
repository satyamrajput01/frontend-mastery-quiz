How the Quiz Works

  • Single-Answer Locking: Selecting one of the four options immediately locks the question to prevent multiple attempts.
  • Scoring and Explanations: Each accurate selection adds 1 point to your live score. Every selection immediately reveals the verified answer and a short technical explanation.
  • Progress and Elapsed Time: A progress bar tracks completion from Question 1 through Question 15 while an elapsed-time counter records your session duration in minutes and seconds.
  • Completion and Review: Finishing Question 15 stops the timer and displays your total score, accuracy percentage, elapsed time, domain breakdown, and a complete answer log.

Exported Results Format

On the results screen, you can download a JSON file generated directly in your browser. The exported file includes the completion timestamp, elapsed duration, overall score and percentage, per-subject tallies, and each question with your selected option, the correct option, and the explanation.
